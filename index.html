Step 1

Paste 5 LinkedIn prospects

Each prospect needs two things: their LinkedIn link and their profile details.

  1. Copy the profile link from your browser's address bar while viewing their LinkedIn profile. It looks like linkedin.com/in/their-name. Paste it into a slot.
  2. Add their profile details. Prospect can't open LinkedIn links by itself, so either: Upload the PDF Recommended
    On the profile click More → Save to PDF, then Upload LinkedIn PDF below.     Or paste the text
    On the profile press Ctrl/⌘ + A, then Ctrl/⌘ + C, and paste into the box.
  3. Repeat for all 5 prospects. A slot turns green when both the link and details are in. Make sure the details match the link — Prospect warns you if they don't seem to.
Why can't Prospect just open the link, or let me sign in with LinkedIn?

LinkedIn profiles sit behind a login, and LinkedIn doesn't let other websites read them. Signing in with LinkedIn only shares your own name and email — not other people's profiles. Copying the profile yourself is the safe, allowed way. Never give any website your LinkedIn password.

Your prospects' details stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.
